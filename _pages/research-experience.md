    <div class="research-card__meta">2025 · Undergraduate thesis</div>
    <h2>Changes in Young People's Mate-Selection Values in China</h2>
    <p class="research-card__role">Project Lead and Thesis Author · Provincial Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program</p>
    <p>Examined changes in young people's mate-selection values through a content analysis of marriage advertisements published since China's Reform and Opening-Up.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Built a corpus of more than 10,000 marriage advertisements.</li>
      <li>Designed the coding scheme and coder-training process and completed the coding of more than 3,000 sampled advertisements.</li>
      <li>Applied Qualitative Comparative Analysis (QCA) to investigate relationships among social change, media discourse, and youth marriage values.</li>
    </ul>
